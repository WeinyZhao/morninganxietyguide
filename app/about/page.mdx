## About Morning Anxiety Guide

Morning Anxiety Guide is a free online resource dedicated to helping people manage morning anxiety through evidence-based breathing techniques and practical calm-down strategies.

## Our Mission

We believe that everyone deserves to start their day feeling calm and in control — without expensive apps, subscriptions, or complicated routines. Our mission is to make simple, science-backed anxiety relief techniques accessible to everyone, for free.

## What We Offer

- **Free Breathing Timers** — Interactive tools like our Box Breathing Timer that guide you through breathing exercises with visual cues and audio prompts. No signup required.

- **Evidence-Based Guides** — Articles written around specific anxiety-relief techniques, backed by research and real-world use. We focus on what actually works.

- **Practical Tips** — Actionable advice you can use immediately, whether you're dealing with occasional morning anxiety or looking for long-term management strategies.

## Our Approach

We review published research on breathing techniques, vagus nerve stimulation, and the parasympathetic nervous system. Our articles cite credible sources and explain the science in plain language.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use trackers beyond basic, privacy-respecting analytics. Our only revenue comes from Google AdSense advertisements.

## The Breathing Techniques We Cover

Our content focuses on well-researched breathing techniques including:

- **Box Breathing** — The 4-4-4-4 pattern used by Navy SEALs and first responders to stay calm under pressure
- **4-7-8 Breathing** — Dr. Andrew Weil's "natural tranquilizer for the nervous system"
- **Physiological Sigh** — Backed by Stanford research, the fastest known way to reduce acute stress
- **Diaphragmatic Breathing** — The foundation of most anxiety-relief breathing practices

## Who Runs This Site

Morning Anxiety Guide is run by a small independent team. We are not affiliated with any medical institution. Our content is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

If you are experiencing severe or persistent anxiety, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
