## Introduction

Morning Anxiety Guide ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website https://morninganxietyguide.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

## Information We Collect

**Automatically Collected Information**
When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Referring website addresses

This information is collected through standard server logs and basic analytics tools.

**Cookies**
We use cookies to understand how you use our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

**Analytics**
We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not collect personally identifiable information. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate and maintain our website
- Understand how visitors use our website
- Improve user experience
- Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

## Third-Party Services

**Google AdSense**
We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

**Google Analytics**
We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic. Google Analytics collects information in a way that does not personally identify you. You can learn more about [Google Analytics privacy practices](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).

## Advertising

We display third-party advertisements through Google AdSense. These ads may be tailored to your interests based on:
- Your general location (country/region level)
- Browser type
- Pages you have visited on our site

We do not share personal information with advertisers.

## Data Retention

Automatically collected server log data is retained for a maximum of 30 days. Analytics data is retained in aggregated, anonymized form.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
